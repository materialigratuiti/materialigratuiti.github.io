# KDP: originalità dei contenuti, low-content, AI e requisiti per un libro da bagno umoristico/enigmistico (IT)

Research date: 2026-11-15. Methodological note: the session's network blocked direct page fetches (kdp.amazon.com, authorsguild.org, vappingo.com all returned "ENOTFOUND"), so KDP help-page content below comes from search-engine extracts of the official pages plus secondary sources. Exact current wording on KDP help pages should be re-verified before publication (KDP updates pages without versioned changelogs).

## 1. KDP rules: low-content books, "disappointing content", public-domain / freely available content

### Takeaway
A mixed book of jokes, short stories, crosswords and word games is NOT a "low-content book" in KDP's definition. That label covers repetitive fill-in books (journals, notebooks, planners). Activity/puzzle books are explicitly excluded, so the book should go through the normal flow with an ISBN (free KDP ISBN is fine), and it gets Look Inside. The real risks are the Content Guidelines on "disappointing content" (too short, content freely available on the web, content not differentiated from other books) and the rule against copyrighted web content not supplied by its owner.

### Cited Findings
- KDP help page (low-content): low-content books are generally repetitive, fill-in-style books (journals, notebooks, planners). Activity books such as puzzle books or coloring books are excluded because they generally do not repeat content on each page. KDP notes there can be exceptions. — [KDP Help GGE5T76TWKA85DJM](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/GGE5T76TWKA85DJM)
- Low-content books do not require an ISBN. They are NOT eligible for the free KDP ISBN: the publisher either uses their own ISBN or publishes without one. — [KDP Help GGE5T76TWKA85DJM](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/GGE5T76TWKA85DJM); [Just Publishing Advice](https://justpublishingadvice.com/low-content-books-on-amazon/); [Kindlepreneur](https://kindlepreneur.com/low-content-books/)
- For low-content books published without an ISBN, the "Look Inside" sample and the back-cover thumbnail are not supported. Guides suggest workarounds such as A+ Content or interior images on the cover. — [KDP Help GGE5T76TWKA85DJM](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/GGE5T76TWKA85DJM); [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/low-content-books-income/)
- The same KDP page covers the low-content checkbox (under Categories) and warns that text or objects placed in the back-cover barcode area can cause a file rejection. — [KDP Help GGE5T76TWKA85DJM](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/GGE5T76TWKA85DJM)
- Content Guidelines: KDP "doesn't allow content that's typically disappointing to customers, including the examples provided in the Guide to Kindle Content Quality". KDP will reject or remove content that doesn't comply and investigates when notified. — [KDP Content Guidelines G200672390](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390)
- Examples of "disappointing content" (as quoted by secondary sources from the Guide to Kindle Content Quality):
  - content freely available on the web (unless you own the copyright or it is public domain)
  - content that is too short
  - poorly translated content
  - content not significantly different from other books
  - content that doesn't make for an enjoyable read
  - misleading content, or content that is primarily advertising
  - content incomplete without an external source

  — [Blood Red Pencil, 2019](https://bloodredpencil.blogspot.com/2019/08/new-writing-tricks-tools-kindle-quality.html); [Vappingo – KDP content guidelines](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-content-guidelines/); official page: [Guide to Kindle Content Quality G200952510](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200952510)
- Freely available web content: KDP "will not accept content under copyright that is freely available on the web unless it's provided by the owner of the copyright". — [KDP Help G200672390](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390)
- Public domain: allowed, but KDP may ask for proof. KDP can refuse public-domain content already available in the store. If a free version exists, only a "differentiated" version (annotations, new translation, etc.) is published. The following do NOT count as differentiation: a linked table of contents, formatting improvements, collections, sales rank, price, or freely available Internet content. Public-domain titles may be excluded from some royalty options and KDP Select. — [KDP Help G200743940 (public domain)](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200743940); [KDP Help G200672390](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390)
- Third-party guides note that republishing public-domain text without added value can lead to removal even when it is legally PD. — [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-content-guidelines/); [Inkfluence AI 2026](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/kdp-rejected-my-book-2026)

### Inferences
- A collection of jokes copied from websites (e.g. barzellette sites, Facebook pages) falls squarely under both "freely available on the web" and "not differentiated" ("collections" do not count as differentiation). That makes rejection or removal plausible even before any legal claim. The safest model is original jokes or substantially rewritten material in the author's own voice, plus original stories and puzzles.
- Do NOT tick "low-content". Use the free KDP ISBN for the paperback so Look Inside works. Look Inside matters for a gift/humor book because buyers want to preview the jokes.
- Look Inside and reviews will expose thin or recycled content. A low-effort mix of web jokes plus generator puzzles also risks negative reviews, which the "disappointing content" framework considers.

### Gaps
- The full current text of the "Guide to Kindle Content Quality" could not be fetched (domain blocked). The examples above come from 2019 and 2025-26 secondary quotes.
- No official KDP statement found on a specific "puzzle book quality" review process. Enforcement appears to rely on complaints and automated checks.

## 2. Copyright of jokes, riddles and crosswords (Italy/EU, US); risks of copying Settimana Enigmistica

### Takeaway
Under both EU/Italian and US law, a joke's idea or punchline concept is free, but its specific wording can be protected if original. Short jokes get "thin" protection, but online jokes are still presumptively protected. Crossword grid layouts and individual clue/answer pairs are weakly protected, but a collection of puzzles (selection and arrangement), longer original clue sets, and a magazine's overall format are protectable. Italian unfair-competition law (art. 2598 c.c., "concorrenza parassitaria") adds risk beyond copyright. Systematic copying from Settimana Enigmistica or any other publisher is high-risk and unnecessary.

### Cited Findings
- US: there is no minimum length for copyright. Jokes fixed in tangible form can be protected, but courts give short jokes "thin" protection (Kaseberg v. Conaco, over jokes allegedly taken for Conan). If a joke can only be expressed in a few ways, it is likely unprotectable (idea/expression merger). — [OSU Libraries Copyright](https://library.osu.edu/site/copyright/tag/comedy/); [NPR 2017](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2017/05/17/528680860/can-you-copyright-your-dumb-joke-and-how-can-you-prove-its-yours); [Above the Law 2017](https://abovethelaw.com/2017/09/surely-you-jest-copyright-and-comedy/)
- US: online content is presumptively protected even without a copyright notice. Being unable to find the owner does not authorise use. Statutory damages run from $750 to $30,000 per infringed work (up to $150,000 if wilful). — [copyrightlaws.com](https://www.copyrightlaws.com/sharing-republishing-online-content/); [Claremont Colleges Library](https://copyright.library.claremont.edu/posting-and-using-items-posted-on-the-web/)
- EU (CJEU, Infopaq C-5/08, 16 July 2009): an extract of 11 words can be a protected partial reproduction if it expresses "the author's own intellectual creation". Single words are not protected, but their "choice, sequence and combination" can be. The national court decides. Later case law: originality means a creation "reflecting his personality and expressing his free and creative choices". — [Wikipedia – Infopaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infopaq_International_A/S_v_Danske_Dagblades_Forening); [Pinsent Masons Out-Law](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/en-gb/out-law/news/eleven-word-snippets-can-infringe-copyright-rules-ecj); [IPPT full text](https://www.ippt.eu/sites/ippt/files/2009/IPPT20090716_ECJ_Infopaq_v_DDF.pdf)
- EU: the Database Directive sets originality ("author's own intellectual creation") as the sole criterion for copyright in compilations. Puzzle collections and anthologies can be protected as compilations. — [Mondaq – harmonisation of originality](https://www.mondaq.com/ip/156532/gradual-harmonisation-of-the-threshold-of-originality)
- Italy (L. 633/1941): protection arises automatically when the work is created as an expression of intellectual work, without registration, provided it is creative. Moral rights (attribution) are inalienable. — [Testo L. 633/1941 – MiC](https://biblioteche.cultura.gov.it/it/documenti/Eventi/2014/0.l_633.pdf); [SAB Toscana – Q&A diritto d'autore](https://sab-toscana.cultura.gov.it/fileadmin/risorse/normativa/Diritto_autore_quesiti_casi_concreti.pdf)
- Crosswords (US): the Copyright Office requires human authorship to register a crossword. Compilations of machine-generated puzzles may be registrable if there is authorship in selecting and creating the clues. — [US Copyright Office, Copyright Lore April 2015](https://www.copyright.gov/history/lore/pdfs/201504%20CLore_April2015.pdf)
- Crosswords: the theme is an idea and is probably unprotectable. A GWU analysis of a crossword plagiarism controversy concludes that reused prompts, solutions, grid layouts and skeletal themes are likely not protected. Plagiarism (copying without attribution) is distinct from infringement. — [Volokh Conspiracy 2016](https://reason.com/2016/03/15/crosswords-and-copyright); [GWU IP Law student brief](https://studentbriefs.law.gwu.edu/gwipel/?p=728)
- Contrary informal view (UK radio legal Q&A): a crossword is protected as soon as it is fixed. This is not authoritative. — [LBC Mystery Hour](https://www.lbc.co.uk/radio/special-shows/the-mystery-hour/law/can-crosswords-be-copywrited/)
- Italy, unfair competition in publishing: the Tribunale di Trento held that republishing articles already published by another magazine is "concorrenza sleale". The Cassazione (ord. 9770/2018) dealt with servile imitation (imitazione servile) between competing magazines. Brescia 2023 described "concorrenza parassitaria" (systematic free-riding, sincronica/diacronica). — [Filodiritto – Trib. Trento](https://www.filodiritto.com/tribunale-di-trento-pubblichi-gli-stessi-articoli-di-unaltra-rivista-ma-pagamento-e-concorrenza-sleale); [Assolombarda – Cass. 9770/18](https://assolombarda.it/servizi/fisco/documenti/corte-di-cassazione-sez-i-civile-ordinanza-n-9770-18); [OBGC Unibs – Trib. Brescia 2378/2023](https://www.obgc.unibs.it/osservatorio/index.php/2023/09/25/tribunale-di-brescia-sentenza-del-25-settembre-2023-n-2378-atti-di-concorrenza-sleale-concorrenza-parassitaria-concorrenza-parassitaria-c-d-diacronica-e-c-d-sincronica/?print=pdf)

### Inferences
- **Jokes:** "Traditional" Italian jokes (the old "Pierino" or "carabinieri" jokes) are folk material with no identifiable author, and the underlying gag is free. The specific wording of a version copied from a website or book may still be someone's expression. Safe practice:
  - rewrite every joke in your own words, setting and characters (e.g. bathroom themes)
  - never copy a whole site's selection or order
  - prefer original jokes
  - keep a log of how each item was written

  This addresses both copyright and KDP's "freely available content" rule, which is applied by automated matching and is stricter than the law.
- **Riddles and indovinelli:** the same logic applies. Classic riddles (e.g. "Cosa ha i denti ma non morde?") are idea-level and widespread. Copy well-known modern authored riddle collections verbatim and you risk both copyright and KDP flags.
- **Settimana Enigmistica:** copying its grids and clues risks three things:
  - copyright in its selection/compilation and in any longer creative clues (Infopaq standard)
  - unfair competition or parasitic competition claims under art. 2598 c.c.
  - trademark issues if its name, logos, puzzle-type names or trade dress are referenced on the cover or in metadata

  KDP will also remove content on a rights-holder complaint. Using generic puzzle *formats* (cruciverba, rebus, crucipuzzle, parole crociate a schema libero) is fine, because formats and ideas are not protected. Do not imitate Settimana Enigmistica's graphic trade dress or use its name in keywords.
- **Crosswords made with generators:** the grid fill is weakly protected. Write the clues yourself, in Italian and with an umorismo/bagno theme, to secure original authorship (US: selection and creation of clues; IT/EU: free creative choices).

### Gaps
- No Italian case law found specifically on barzellette or on Settimana Enigmistica litigation (the searches returned nothing). The Italian joke analysis above is inference from general principles.
- The sui generis database right (Dir. 96/9/EC; L. 633/1941 art. 102-bis) was not confirmed by a found source in this session. In principle it protects substantial investment in collections (e.g. a magazine's puzzle archive) independently of originality. This needs separate verification.

## 3. KDP AI-generated content disclosure; AI in covers and interiors; copyright status of AI output (US/EU/IT)

### Takeaway
KDP requires disclosure of "AI-generated" content when publishing a new title (or editing one). That means text, images or translations created by an AI tool, even if heavily edited afterwards. "AI-assisted" content needs no disclosure: content you created yourself and only edited, refined or checked with AI. AI images and text are allowed in covers and interiors provided you disclose them and they don't infringe. A cap of 3 new titles per day per account has been in place since September 2023. Purely AI-generated material is not copyrightable in the US (USCO Jan 2025 report; Thaler v. Perlmutter, D.C. Cir., 18 March 2025). Italy's AI law (L. 132/2025, in force 10 Oct 2025) protects works made "with the aid of" AI only if they result from the human author's intellectual work.

### Cited Findings
- KDP Content Guidelines (current help-page wording):
  - **AI-generated:** "If you used an AI-based tool to create the actual content (whether text, images, or translations), it is considered 'AI-generated,' even if you applied substantial edits afterwards."
  - **AI-assisted:** content you created yourself and then edited, refined or error-checked with AI. "You are not required to disclose AI-assisted content."
  - You remain responsible for IP compliance in both cases.

  — [KDP Content Guidelines G200672390](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200672390); [KDP Help G200672410](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200672410)
- The 2023 version (quoted): "We require you to inform us of AI-generated content (text, images, or translations) when you publish a new book or make edits to and republish an existing book through KDP." AI-generated images explicitly included "cover and interior images and artwork". The current snippets seen in this session did not show the cover-specific line, so verify on the live page. — [Goodreads blog quoting KDP, 2023](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/24055598-amazon-kdp-new-policy-on-generative-ai?tab=book); [LessWrong – KDP AI guidelines](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/4csaBfAgiyzrFXvEj/amazon-kdp-ai-content-guidelines)
- Disclosure is made per format: a compliance guide says that if you publish both an ebook and a paperback with AI content, you disclose for each. AI-assisted writing does not require disclosure. — [ScribeCount – KDP AI Content Policy compliance guide](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/protecting-books/kdp-ai-content-policy-the-compliance-guide)
- Daily cap:
  - On 18 Sept 2023, KDP announced on its forum a limit of 3 new titles per day per account, citing AI concerns. Amazon told the Guardian the number could be adjusted "if needed", and users hitting the limit are notified.
  - A 2025-26 secondary source says the limit is still 3/day and that AI-disclosure enforcement escalated in 2025-2026. This was not confirmed on an official page.

  — [Publishers Weekly](https://www2.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/digital/content-and-e-books/article/93207-kdp-will-limit-daily-number-of-new-titles.html); [ALLi](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/kdp-limits-titles/); [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/); [ScribeCount](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/protecting-books/KDP%20AI%20Content%20Policy%20)
- US Copyright Office, Part 2 report "Copyrightability" (29 Jan 2025):
  - Existing law suffices, so no new legislation is recommended.
  - "Prompts alone do not provide sufficient human control", and iterating prompts doesn't change this.
  - Human expressive inputs and creative selection/arrangement of AI output can be protected.
  - "Where AI merely assists an author in the creative process, its use does not change the copyrightability of the output."

  — [Copyright Alliance summary](https://copyrightalliance.org/ai-report-part-2-copyrightability/); [Sidley](https://datamatters.sidley.com/2025/02/07/u-s-copyright-office-issues-report-on-artificial-intelligence-and-copyrightability/); [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/usco-releases-part-2-its-report-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence)
- Thaler v. Perlmutter: the D.C. Circuit (18 March 2025, unanimous) affirmed the refusal to register a work autonomously generated by AI, so human authorship is required. — [DLA Piper, March 2025](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-hu/insights/publications/2025/03/copyrightability-of-genai-outputs-in-the-us-key-developments)
- Italy, Legge 23 settembre 2025 n. 132 (GU 25 Sept 2025, in force 10 Oct 2025):
  - Art. 25 amends art. 1 of L. 633/1941: protected works are those "dell'ingegno umano" of creative character, "anche laddove create con l'ausilio di strumenti di intelligenza artificiale, purché costituenti risultato del lavoro intellettuale dell'autore".
  - It also adds art. 70-septies, on text and data mining for AI training.

  — [Lavori Pubblici](https://www.lavoripubblici.it/news/intelligenza-artificiale-pubblicata-gazzetta-ufficiale-legge-ai-132-2025-36444); [IusLetter, 22.10.2025](https://iusletter.com/dalla-redazione/diritto-dellimpresa/diritto-dautore/diritto-dautore-e-opere-create-con-lai/); [Agenda Digitale](https://agendadigitale.eu/cultura-digitale/legge-sullai-come-si-applica-tutti-gli-aspetti-pratici-e-i-fronti-critici)
- EU AI Act (Reg. 2024/1689) Art. 50 transparency obligations:
  - They apply from 2 August 2026, with a grace period to 2 December 2026 for systems already on the market (AI Omnibus) for the marking obligation.
  - Deployers publishing AI-generated text "to inform the public on matters of public interest" must disclose, unless the text has undergone human review and someone holds editorial responsibility. The Commission's July 2026 guidelines read that exception narrowly (superficial review doesn't qualify).
  - Penalties reach up to €15M or 3% of turnover.

  — [Cooley, 3 Aug 2026](https://cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-03-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-take-effect-2-august-2026); [Greenberg Traurig, June 2026](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2026/6/deepfakes-chatbots-ai-generated-text-european-commission-details-transparency-obligations-under-the-ai-act); [artificialintelligenceact.eu – Art. 50 guide](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/transparency-rules-article-50/); [William Fry](https://www.williamfry.com/knowledge/time-running-out-as-ai-transparency-guidelines-published/)

### Inferences
- **For this book:** if jokes, stories or images are generated by an AI and then edited, KDP classifies them as AI-generated and you must tick "yes" in the AI questions for each format. If you write the jokes and stories yourself and use AI only for proofreading, brainstorming or checking, that is AI-assisted and needs no disclosure. Grey zone: AI proposes 50 jokes and you pick and rewrite them. KDP's "even if you applied substantial edits" wording points toward disclosure. Disclosing carries no listed penalty, while non-disclosure risks account action, so disclose when in doubt.
- **Puzzles:** crossword and word-search grids from a deterministic generator are probably not "AI-based tools" in KDP's sense. AI-written clue lists would be AI-generated text. This is an interpretation, not confirmed by KDP.
- **Copyright of the book:** AI-generated jokes or images are likely unprotected (US) or protected only to the extent of human creative contribution (IT/EU, L. 132/2025 and the Infopaq originality test). Competitors could therefore copy those parts freely. Human-written jokes, clues and stories, plus the human selection and arrangement of the whole book, remain protectable.
- **AI Act Art. 50:** its text-disclosure duty targets public-interest informational text. A humor/puzzle book is unlikely to fall in that category. AI-generated images that are "deep fakes" (realistic depictions of real people) would trigger disclosure, so avoid caricatures of real people generated with AI. This is inference; the guidelines were not read in full.
- **AI covers:** AI covers are allowed if disclosed. Avoid generating images "in the style of" or containing recognisable trademarks or characters.

### Gaps
- The live KDP page could not be fetched to confirm whether the "cover and interior images" wording is still present in 2026, or whether disclosure triggers any visible label on the product page (as of the sources found, disclosure is not shown to customers, but this was not verified this session).
- No official 2025-26 source found confirming that the 3/day cap is unchanged.

## 4. Puzzle book quality expectations and KDP paperback interior requirements

### Takeaway
KDP paperbacks need at least 24 pages (even page count), 0.125" bleed only if elements touch the edge, and margins that scale with page count. Puzzle books are expected to have a complete, accurate answer key and legible grids. No official KDP font-size minimum for puzzles was found.

### Cited Findings
- Minimum page count is 24. The maximum depends on ink, paper and trim. — [KDP Help – Trim size / page count G201834260](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G201834260); [KDP Help GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6)
- The page count must be even because books print in spreads. — [Vappingo – manuscript formatting](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-manuscript-formatting-requirements/)
- Margins (secondary sources quoting the KDP table):

  | Item | Value |
  |---|---|
  | Gutter, 24–150 pages | 0.375" |
  | Gutter, 151–300 pages | 0.5" |
  | Gutter, 301–500 pages | 0.625" |
  | Outside/top/bottom, no bleed | ≥0.25" |
  | Outside/top/bottom, with bleed | ≥0.375" |

  Another source gives coarser bands that conflict with these, so check KDP's live table. — [Vappingo – bleed & safe zones](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-bleed-safe-zones/); [ScribeCount – print formatting](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/publishing-a-book/Print-Book-Formatting-Requirements-for-Self-Published-Authors); [KDPBuilder](https://kdpbuilder.com/blog/kdp-manuscript-formatting-trim-margins-bleed)
- Bleed is 0.125" beyond the trim. If even one page needs bleed, the whole file must be set up with bleed. Set the trim size before the margins. — [Vappingo – bleed](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-bleed-safe-zones/); [Univers Studio bleed guide](https://univers.studio/bleed-guide/)
- Puzzle-book practice (vendor guides, not KDP policy):
  - A complete answer key is expected, and a mismatched key undermines trust in the whole book. Verify every solution rather than trusting the generator.
  - Unsolvable or duplicated puzzles and false "large print" claims are common review complaints.
  - Large print means body ≥14 pt, or for puzzles, grid letters ≥18 pt and clues ≥14 pt.

  — [KDPBuilder – word search 2026](https://kdpbuilder.com/blog/word-search-book-publishing-2026-kdp-guide); [KDPBuilder – puzzle book guide 2026](https://kdpbuilder.com/blog/puzzle-book-publishing-complete-kdp-guide-2026); [PuzzleForge, May 2026](https://puzzleforge-907.pages.dev/how-to-make-word-search-book-kdp)

### Inferences
- **Trim and paper:** for a bathroom book a compact trim (e.g. 6"x9" or 5.5"x8.5") with no bleed simplifies layout. Keep grids inside the margins.
- **Paper and ink:** white paper with black ink is the cheapest. Pencil and pen solving favours non-glossy interiors, which is standard for KDP B&W.
- **Grid legibility:** at 6x9, crossword cells of at least about 7–8 mm with clue numbers readable at 7–8 pt are a reasonable target. Answer keys can use reduced grids. This is a design inference, not a KDP rule.
- **Mixed content:** the joke and story sections make "too short" less of a concern. Still, a 24–40 page book at a premium price risks "disappointing" reviews.

### Gaps
- No official KDP minimum font size or puzzle-specific quality checklist was found.
- The exact current margin table was not fetched from KDP.

## 5. Tools for creating original crosswords and word searches, and the licensing of their output

### Takeaway
Many generators exist (free web tools, paid desktop software, KDP-oriented Gumroad tools). Output rights depend entirely on each tool's licence. Some free tools forbid commercial use, while many paid KDP tools grant full commercial rights. Read the licence before publishing, avoid PLR puzzle packs (which create duplicate-content risk), and write your own clues.

### Cited Findings
- EclipseCrossword (free Windows crossword maker) publishes a licence FAQ, "Is EclipseCrossword really free?", that covers use of the puzzles created. Read it for commercial-use terms. — [EclipseCrossword license FAQ](https://eclipsecrossword.com/help/license)
- Paid KDP-oriented crossword tools are sold with commercial licences, e.g. a $39 one-time-fee tool allowing sale of the crosswords created. Others state "full commercial rights to the content you generate". — [Bowes Publishing mini crossword generator](https://bowespublishing.gumroad.com/l/minicrosswordgenerator); [Bearwood Labs Crossword Creator](https://bearwoodlabs.gumroad.com/l/crossword-creator); [KDPBuilder AI crossword creator](https://kdpbuilder.com/ai-crossword-book-creator)
- Some free online generators state that commercial use is allowed. A KDP guide warns that some free tools prohibit it and advises reading the terms. — [FreeToolsKit crossword generator](https://freetoolskit.pages.dev/kdp-designer/crossword-generator/); [KDPEasy – best word search generator](https://www.kdpeasy.com/blog/best-word-search-generator-kdp)
- Bundled suites exist (Self Publishing Titans Puzzle & Activity Bundle). PLR puzzle packs allow use in products but forbid reselling the files. — [Self Publishing Titans](https://www.selfpublishingtitans.com/puzzle-activity-bundle-1-sales); [Getly PLR pack](https://www.getly.store/product/plr-puzzle-pack-for-kdp-220-new-sudoku-mazes-and-word-search-with-commercial-lic-mv04tuta)

### Inferences
- PLR packs are sold to many buyers, so the same puzzles appear in many KDP books. That collides with the "not significantly different from other books" disappointing-content example. Avoid them for this book.
- **Italian-language book:** most generators ship English dictionaries. Supply your own Italian word lists (bathroom- and humor-themed) and write the clues yourself. This makes the content original, fits the niche, and maximises copyright protection.
- **AI-generated output:** if a tool marketed as "AI-powered" writes clues or word lists, treat that output as AI-generated for KDP disclosure.
- **Licence records:** keep a copy or screenshot of each tool's licence terms at the date of use.

### Gaps
- Crossword Compiler's licence terms (a widely used professional tool) were not found in this session.
- No Italian-specific crossword generator with verified licence terms was found.
- The vendor licence claims above are marketing-page statements, not reviewed licence texts.
