# 3. Descrizione per Amazon (in inglese)

Incolla il blocco qui sotto nella casella "Description" di KDP (accetta questi tag HTML semplici).

```html
<p><b>The clockmaker is dead. Fifty villagers have something to hide. And you are the only one who can solve it.</b></p>

<p>Welcome to Marrowby, a sleepy English village where the tea is strong, the gossip is stronger, and every clock has stopped at seven minutes past midnight. Constable Harcourt needs a sharp mind from outside the village, and that is you.</p>

<p><b>How it works:</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Read the case.</b> Each left-hand page tells a short cozy mystery with three clues.</li>
<li><b>Solve the puzzle.</b> Each right-hand page has a 15x15 word search with 15 words to find, in every direction, even backwards.</li>
<li><b>Find the hidden word.</b> A 16th word is buried in the grid and is not on the list. Use the clues to guess it.</li>
<li><b>Name the culprit.</b> After 50 cases, your 50 clue words combine to reveal who did it.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Inside this book:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>50 village mysteries, each with its own setting, from the vicarage kitchen to the old windmill</li>
<li>800 hidden words, with no word ever repeated</li>
<li>A cast of eleven suspects and a friendly constable</li>
<li>An Evidence Board to track your clue words</li>
<li>A final case and deduction page</li>
<li>Complete solutions for every puzzle</li>
<li>6 x 9 inch size, easy to carry on a train, in a garden chair or on a rainy afternoon</li>
</ul>

<p>No gore, no rush, no horror. Just a cup of tea, a sharp pencil, and a village full of secrets. A thoughtful gift for fans of village whodunits, word searches and classic detective puzzles.</p>

<p><b>Scroll up and click "Buy now" to start your first case.</b></p>
```

Note:
- Ho scritto solo cose vere per questo libro: 50 casi, 800 parole (50 x 16), 15x15, 6x9, soluzioni incluse. Il numero di sospettati è 11 (il cast ha 13 nomi: la vittima e il constable non sono sospettati). Se cambi il cast, aggiorna queste righe.
- Non ci sono frasi come "bestseller", "il numero uno" o recensioni inventate.
- Non c'è "large print".
